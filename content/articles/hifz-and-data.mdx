Anecdotally, teachers have long believed that structured Qur'anic memorisation builds discipline that
carries over into other subjects. Turning that belief into evidence requires proper measurement.

## The study design

The analysis draws on assessment records and memorisation progress logs, using descriptive statistics as a
first step before correlational testing. Aggregate frequency data has limits, and any claim of causation
would be premature at this stage.

## Early findings

Students with consistent daily memorisation targets showed steadier attendance and completion rates on
other coursework, though the sample and design cannot yet separate memorisation itself from the discipline
of the surrounding programme.

## What comes next

The next phase moves from aggregate frequencies to respondent-level records, which will allow proper
Pearson correlation and chi-square testing rather than descriptive comparison alone.
