The SDG 16 outreach programme in Aboru did not begin with a curriculum. It began with listening: to
parents, to young people, and to the disagreements already present in the community.

## Why the mosque was the right starting point

A mosque carries trust that a new institution has to earn. Building the programme inside an existing,
trusted space meant families engaged from week one rather than after months of relationship-building.

## What the programme actually does

Weekly sessions combine short Islamic guidance on conflict and patience with structured peer mediation
practice, run by trained youth volunteers rather than only by adults.

## A caution for other organisers

Peacebuilding work is slow and difficult to quantify. Resist the pressure to report only what is easy to
count; the honest picture includes setbacks alongside the outreach numbers.
